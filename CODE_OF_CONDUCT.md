# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about code, not about people.

## What we expect

- Assume the other person is trying to help, and read their message that way
  first.
- Criticise the work, specifically. "This drops audio during a rate change" is
  useful; "this is sloppy" is not.
- Accept that you might be wrong, and say so when you are.
- Respect a maintainer's decision not to take a change. Scope is a real
  constraint, and "no" is not an insult.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind, including about someone's
  background, identity, or level of experience.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Sustained disruption: repeatedly reopening settled arguments, or making the
  project unpleasant to participate in.

Nobody has to justify why a behaviour bothered them for it to be worth
stopping.

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages — and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **conduct@classeve.com**. Reports are read by ClassEve and
kept confidential; we will not discuss a report with the person it concerns
without telling you first.

We will respond in proportion to what happened: usually a private word, which
is enough almost every time. Where it is not, that escalates to a public
correction, a temporary block, or a permanent one. If a maintainer is the
problem, the same process applies to them.
